# Pixel Art Card Icons

Here are the custom-designed pixel art icons for your upgrade cards. These have been designed with a clean, minimalist 16-bit aesthetic to match the game's style without being overly complex.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **How to use these icons in your game:**
> 1. Download these images to your computer.
> 2. Open **Roblox Studio**.
> 3. Go to the **Creator Hub** (or use the Asset Manager in Studio).
> 4. Upload these images as **Decals**.
> 5. Once approved, copy the **Asset ID** (e.g., `rbxassetid://123456789`).
> 6. Send me the IDs, and I will update `UpgradeConfig.lua` for you!

### Icon Descriptions:
*   **Fireball:** A classic glowing projectile for the Fireball weapon.
*   **Crit Chance:** A yellow crosshair representing precision and critical hits.
*   **Crit Multiplier:** A glowing golden sword for massive critical damage.
*   **Lifesteal:** A red droplet with a heart, symbolizing health gained from combat.
*   **XP Boost:** A purple magical scroll representing knowledge and fast leveling.
*   **Area Size:** A blue expanding circle symbol for AoE upgrades.
*   **Lightning:** A sharp blue/yellow bolt for the Chain Lightning weapon.
*   **Snowball:** A frosty white snowball for the freezing weapon.
